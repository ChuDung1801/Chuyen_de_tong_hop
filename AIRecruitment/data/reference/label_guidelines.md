# Label guidelines (draft for Sprint 1)

Before creating labels, the team must agree on an operational definition of a relevant CV–Job pair. Labels should be based on documented criteria and reviewed examples, not arbitrary guesses.

Suggested first draft:
- `1` — evidence in the profile supports the mandatory skills and minimum experience requirements stated in the job.
- `0` — one or more mandatory requirements are clearly not met based on available evidence.
- `uncertain` — insufficient evidence; exclude from binary training until adjudicated.

Do not use protected characteristics or proxies as matching features. Keep an annotation log and measure agreement between reviewers.
